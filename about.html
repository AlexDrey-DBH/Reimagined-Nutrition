Origin Story

The kitchen came first.
The clinical training came from caring for someone.

After graduating from the CIA, Ali became a caregiver to a terminally ill loved one. Working alongside his oncology dietitian, she put those recommendations into practice in her own kitchen and saw how food could improve the quality of life for even the most critically ill people.

Shortly after, Ali enrolled in a Bachelor's program to get the training required to become a Registered Dietitian herself.

That is why Reimagined Nutrition does not separate taste from nutrition.
A plan only works if the food works. A meal only helps if someone wants to eat it.
