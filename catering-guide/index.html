Concierge Culinary Wellness in Bergen County and Beyond

About Reimagined Nutrition

Reimagined Nutrition is a concierge culinary wellness company led by Ali Senatore, RDN, CDN, an Executive Chef and Registered Dietitian Nutritionist. The company caters private, corporate and full service events across Bergen County, North Jersey, New York City, Westchester and Long Island, for parties of a few guests up to 500.

Every menu is reviewed by a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist for your guests' dietary needs, every dish is labeled for allergens, and every event is staffed by our own trained team. Menus are built around local growers, seasonal ingredients, and the freshest local meat and seafood.

  • Catering. Private, corporate and full service events up to 500 guests.
  • Private chef dinners. Plated, coursed dinners in your home.
  • Office lunch programs. Weekly deliveries on a rotating chef's menu.
  • Culinary programming. Adult and kids cooking classes at all levels, private in home lessons, corporate wellness workshops and lunch and learns, community demos, pop ups and farmers markets.
  • Local partners. Goffle Farms chicken and duck, microgreens custom grown by Vert Farmer to pair with each dish on your menu, mozzarella handmade by The Salty Cow in Bloomfield and picked up the morning of your event, honey from Uncle Gordon's Backyard Honey in North Bergen, and custom cakes by CIA Executive Pastry Chef Melissa Mak.
  • Less waste. Compostable disposables available. When you would like the remaining food donated, we gladly coordinate delivery to our local food pantry partners, so nothing good goes to waste.
  • Ownership. A woman owned and operated business.

Lead Chef

Meet the Chef: Ali Senatore, RDN, CDN

Ali Senatore is an Executive Chef and Registered Dietitian Nutritionist, bringing a chef’s palate and a clinician’s precision to every plate. She trained at the Culinary Institute of America and cooked in two Michelin recognized New York City kitchens, mentored under Michelin recognized chefs, before earning her credentials as a Registered Dietitian. She has cooked professionally since 2013.

Ali has served as a Food Service Director in New York City, ran a $6 million college dining program, and led teams of up to 65. Her allergen and nutrition programming contributed to collegiate NACUFS awards, and she has earned two ACF silver medals in team culinary competition.

From 2016 to 2018 she was the VIP concert lounge chef at PNC Bank Arts Center and The Stone Pony, cooking directly for touring artists, their crews and their families. Clients she has cooked for include Bon Jovi, Stevie Nicks, Joan Jett, the Jonas Brothers, Bruce Springsteen, Robert De Niro and Daniel Radcliffe.

Choosing Your Service

Catering Service Styles

Drop-off

Delivered ready to serve in disposable trays with labels and serving utensils. No staff on site.

Best for offices, meetings and casual gatherings.

Staffed buffet

Our team sets up, keeps the buffet fresh and full, and cleans up after.

Best for showers, birthdays and corporate events.

Family style

Shared platters served to each table so guests pass and help themselves.

Best for smaller parties that want a warm, communal feel.

Grazing and stations

Abundant displays and interactive stations guests return to all evening.

Best for receptions and cocktail hours.

Passed

Event Servers circulate with hors d'oeuvres so guests can mingle freely.

Best for larger receptions.

Plated

Coursed dinner, each plate finished in the kitchen and served to the table. Plated service $5 each.

Best for private dinners, recommended under 15 guests.

We own every event, start to finish, and staff it entirely with our own team. It's how we protect our standards and leave no room for error. Our bartenders are TIPS certified.

Yes. Every menu is reviewed by a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist, every dish is labeled for allergens, and top 9 allergen free menus are available. Menus are marked vegan, vegetarian, gluten free and dairy free.

Do you offer kosher or halal catering?

Halal meats and kosher style menus are available on request. Kosher style means no pork or shellfish and no meat served with dairy. We are not kosher certified.

Where do you deliver?

We cater across Bergen County, North Jersey, New York City, Westchester and Long Island. Delivery starts at $10 in Bergen County, and New York City events have a $75 delivery fee plus a $50 transport fee.
