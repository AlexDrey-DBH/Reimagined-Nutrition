Caregiving Meal Support

Meal plans for aging parents and spouses.

When food becomes part of caring for someone you love, the everyday decisions can add up quickly. Reimagined Nutrition helps make meals feel more manageable, personal, and nourishing.

Ali Senatore is both an Executive Chef and Registered Dietitian Nutritionist (RDN, CDN), bringing thoughtful meal planning and chef-prepared food together for households across Bergen County NJ, North Jersey, Rockland County NY, and New York City.

The Caregiving Load

One less daily decision to carry.

Maybe your parent is coming home after a hospital stay. Maybe your spouse's appetite, energy, or dietary needs have changed. Or maybe you simply want to know they have meals they will genuinely want to eat.

Ali starts with the person, not a preset menu. That means considering health guidance, food preferences, routines, portions, texture, allergies, and the people helping at home. The goal is practical support that respects both the plan and the pleasure of eating.

What Support Can Include

Food that meets the moment.

A personalized food direction

A focused nutrition assessment and meal plan built around the needs, preferences, routines, and household behind the referral.

Chef-prepared meals

Prepared meals can be designed to make regular eating easier while keeping the food recognizable, satisfying, and personal.

Ongoing adjustments

As schedules, preferences, care needs, or recommendations change, Ali can adjust the next meals and keep the plan workable.

Chef + RDN

Meals that support care without feeling clinical.

Ali brings the clinical awareness of an RDN and the standards of a professional chef to the same table. Her work can account for health needs without losing sight of flavor, comfort, and the foods that still feel familiar.

A meal plan should help you care for someone, not give you another binder to manage.

Common Questions

Caregiving support, made clearer.

Can I reach out for a parent or spouse?

Yes. Many families begin the conversation on behalf of the person receiving meals. Ali will use the questionnaire and call to understand who should be involved and what support is appropriate.

Can this include both meal planning and delivery?

Yes. After learning more about the situation, Ali can recommend a written plan, recurring chef-prepared meals, nutrition counseling, or a combination.
