What A Session Is

Focused, practical, and personal.

Nutrition counseling can support medical nutrition therapy, recovery and post-surgery needs, digestive concerns, allergies and sensitivities, weight and metabolic goals, family meal structure, and general food planning.

The work is grounded in real nutrition expertise and Ali's professional kitchen background. You leave with guidance that connects to what you actually eat, not advice that stays abstract.

Initial sessions run 60 minutes. Follow-ups run 45 minutes.

What You Walk Away With

A clearer food direction.

In your first session Ali collects your medical history, reviews your labwork, identifies nutrition deficits, and makes recommendations. Afterward you receive a complete PDF booklet built for you: a 7-day meal plan, your nutrition assessment, and guides for where to find any better-for-you products that make managing meals simpler at home.

Ali can also provide a one-page outpatient visit summary for your care team, listing the nutrition parameters being monitored, her recommendations, and any concerns or suggested labs for your MD to order.
